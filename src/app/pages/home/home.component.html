FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CAPS-AI? +

CAPS-AI is a business management platform designed to connect customer management, support, inventory, attendance, billing and other operational workflows.

What modules are available? +

CAPS-AI includes CRM, Technical Support, Feedback, Visitor Management, Inventory, POS, AI Attendance, IPOD, AI Bill Automation and eKYC.

Does CAPS-AI support AI Attendance? +

The CAPS-AI platform includes AI Attendance capabilities using face recognition, GPS and geo-fencing.

What is AI Bill Automation? +

AI Bill Automation is designed to process invoices using OCR, AI validation and approval workflows.

How can I request a demo? +
